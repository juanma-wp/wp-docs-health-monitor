Positives

  • The block_editor_settings_all filter hook name and its two parameters ($settings, $context as WP_Block_Editor_Context) are accurately documented and confirmed by the firing site at src/wp-includes/block-editor.php L645: $editor_settings = apply_filters( 'block_editor_settings_all', $editor_settings, $block_editor_context ).
  • The four wp_theme_json_data_* filter hook names (wp_theme_json_data_default, wp_theme_json_data_blocks, wp_theme_json_data_theme, wp_theme_json_data_user) are all confirmed present and firing in src/wp-includes/class-wp-theme-json-resolver.php at lines 175, 425, 292, and 564/593 respectively.
  • GAP: The blockEditor.useSetting.before filter (documented in the JS section) passes settingValue as undefined on its first invocation (not the current resolved value), as seen in get-block-settings.js L118: applyFilters('blockEditor.useSetting.before', undefined, path, clientId, blockName). The doc's example correctly returns settingValue as a fallback, but developers should know the initial value passed is always undefined — the filter is purely for override, not for reading the current value.
